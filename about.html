About Halden

Halden Systems B.V. was founded in Amsterdam in 2018 by people who spent years planning shifts on factory floors with spreadsheets. Since 2024 the company has been backed by Nordwind Capital, a European growth equity firm.

How we are organised

Three divisions. Product & Technology (engineering, product and data) is about half of the company. Commercial covers sales, customer success and marketing. G&A covers finance and people.
