Plan every shift. Know every labour euro.

Halden is workforce planning software for mid-market manufacturers and logistics companies. Plan shifts, forecast labour cost and track overtime across every site, in one place.

Shift planning

Build rosters per site and line, matched to demand forecasts, skills and labour rules in each country.

Labour cost forecasting

See next quarter's labour cost before it happens, including overtime, agency staff and allowances.

Overtime control

Track overtime against budget by site, and catch the drift in week two instead of at month end.

Who uses Halden

More than 140 manufacturers and logistics operators across the Netherlands, Germany, Poland, the UK and Iberia, typically with 500 to 5,000 hourly workers across several sites.

Customers buy Halden as an annual SaaS subscription, priced per site and billed in euros, with implementation services sold alongside.
